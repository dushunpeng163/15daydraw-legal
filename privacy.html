Privacy Policy

App: Sketch a Day (also known as 15-Day Draw / 简笔画·每一天) · Effective Date: 2026-08-21 · Last Updated: 2026-08-21

中文版 · ← Legal index

Summary

Sketch a Day collects no personal information and transmits no data whatsoever. The app works entirely offline. It has no user accounts, shows no advertising, contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and makes no network connections. Everything you create or configure in the app stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time from Settings.

1. Who We Are

Sketch a Day ("we", "us", or "the app") is a drawing-practice app operated by an independent developer. For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: dushunpeng163@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store on servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Account credentials or social media identifiers (the app has no account system)
  • Geographic location data
  • Photos, videos, or microphone input
  • Contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data
  • Any identifier linked to a real person

The app requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, or photo library access).

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is removed when you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app:

  • Practice progress (completed exercises, scores, practice dates)
  • Artworks you draw (saved as images inside the app's private storage)
  • App preferences (language, sound settings)
  • An optional nickname, if you choose to enter one (shown only inside the app as a signature on your artwork; it is never transmitted)
  • A record of your consent to this policy

4. No Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising networks, and no social media integrations. The app makes no network requests during use.

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. The store platforms may collect aggregate install statistics under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google); we receive no personal information from them.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Delete Local Data inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all data. Because we hold no data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is rated for ages 8 and up. We do not knowingly — or unknowingly — collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app collects and transmits no data at all, no personal information from children can be collected, in line with COPPA (United States), GDPR-K (European Union), and equivalent regulations.

7. Your Rights

Under regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA you have rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data on any server, these rights are fully satisfied by the local deletion control described in Section 5. If you have any question, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices (for example, adding an online feature in a future version), we will update this policy, change the "Last Updated" date above, and ask for your consent again inside the app before any new data practice takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns: dushunpeng163@gmail.com
