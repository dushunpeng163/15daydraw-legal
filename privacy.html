Privacy Policy

App: 15-Day Draw · Effective Date: 2026-06-01 · Last Updated: 2026-06-01

中文版 · ← Legal index

Summary

15-Day Draw does not collect any personally identifiable information from children or any user. All drawing progress is stored locally on your device only. We do not show advertising, do not use third-party analytics for marketing, and do not enable social features or external links accessible to children without a parental gate.

1. Who We Are

15-Day Draw ("we", "us", or "the app") is a free educational drawing app designed for children. This app is operated by an independent developer. For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: dushunpeng163@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Account credentials, login data, or social media identifiers
  • Geographic location data
  • Photos, videos, or microphone input
  • Contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Any identifier linked to a real person

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device, never transmitted off the device, and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Drawing progress (which patterns have been completed, star scores)
  • App settings (language preference, audio on/off)
  • A randomly generated local device identifier (used only to encrypt save data on this device)

4. Diagnostic Data (Crash Reports)

To diagnose crashes and improve app stability, we use Sentry (sentry.io) to collect:

  • Crash logs and stack traces when the app crashes
  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Performance metrics (frame rate, load time)

This data is not linked to any user identifier, contains no personal information, IP address collection is disabled, and is used solely to fix bugs and improve app quality. Sentry's privacy practices are described at sentry.io/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

This app is designed for children and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation Article 8 (GDPR-K):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR-K, member-state dependent).
  • The app contains no behavioral advertising, no targeted ads of any kind, and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • External links, settings menus, and any in-app purchase flows (if added in the future) are protected by a parental gate requiring an arithmetic answer.
  • There are no social features, chat, user-generated content sharing, or community functionality.

6. Parental Rights

Since the app collects no personal information, there is no personal data to review, modify, or request deletion of. To remove all locally stored game progress, parents may uninstall the app or use system settings (Settings → Apps → 15-Day Draw → Delete App) to clear all data.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Sentry for crash diagnostics (see Section 4). We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, Adjust, AdMob, Unity Ads, or any other advertising/analytics/social SDK.

8. Data Security

Local save data is encrypted on the device. Crash data sent to Sentry is transmitted over HTTPS. We follow industry-standard practices to protect any data in transit.

9. International Users

The app is available globally. Diagnostic data sent to Sentry may be processed in the United States or European Union. By using the app you consent to such transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated. Material changes will be reflected through an updated effective date and, where appropriate, via in-app notice on first launch after update.

11. Contact

For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact dushunpeng163@gmail.com.
