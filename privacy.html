Privacy Policy

App: Sketch a Day (also known as 15-Day Draw / 简笔画·每一天) · Effective Date: 2026-08-21 · Last Updated: 2026-08-21

中文版 · ← Legal index

Summary

We ourselves collect no personal information. The app has no user accounts, and everything you create (progress, artworks, settings) is stored only on your device. The app is free and supported by advertising: ads are provided by Google AdMob and are shown only after you finish an exercise — never while you are drawing. The advertising SDK may process certain device data as described in Section 4. You can delete all local data at any time from Settings.

1. Who We Are

Sketch a Day ("we", "us", or "the app") is a drawing-practice app operated by an independent developer. For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: dushunpeng163@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store on servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Account credentials or social media identifiers (the app has no account system)
  • Precise geographic location data
  • Photos, videos, or microphone input
  • Contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • Usage analytics or profiling data of our own

The app requests no device permissions other than, on iOS, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt described in Section 4.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your device. We never transmit it, and it is removed when you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app:

  • Practice progress (completed exercises, scores, practice dates)
  • Artworks you draw (saved as images inside the app's private storage)
  • App preferences (language, sound settings)
  • An optional nickname, if you choose to enter one (shown only inside the app as a signature on your artwork; it is never transmitted)
  • A record of your consent to this policy

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small number of ads — an occasional interstitial after you finish an exercise, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for extra hints. Ads never appear while you are drawing.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device data such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), subject to your consent
  • IP address (which may imply coarse location) and device/technical information
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

How Google uses this data is described in Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • iOS: the system App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you deny tracking; if you deny, ads are served without the advertising identifier (non-personalized).
  • EEA / UK: a consent dialog (Google UMP) is shown on first launch; you can decline personalized ads and change your choice later in Settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings ("Privacy → Ads").

We receive only aggregate, non-identifying revenue reports from AdMob — never data about individual users.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Delete Local Data inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data. We hold no data about you on any server of our own. For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, see the Google links in Section 4.

6. Children

The app's content is suitable for ages 8 and up, but the app is not directed at children and is listed in app stores for users aged 13 and over. We ourselves collect no personal information from any user. If you are below the age of digital consent in your region, please use the app with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises privacy concerns, contact us and we will help.

7. Your Rights

Under regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA you have rights to access, correct, and delete personal data. We hold no personal data about you on our servers; local data is fully under your control (Section 5). For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise choices via the controls in Section 4 and Google's My Ad Center.

8. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will update this policy, change the "Last Updated" date above, and where required ask for your consent again inside the app.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns: dushunpeng163@gmail.com
